    Since 1998

    Named for Shantai. Built for everyone else.

    Most old-age homes in Belagavi thirty years ago opened for one community. Vijay More and Vijay Patil opened one for whoever had nowhere left to go.

    A mother, a mayor, a courtyard.

    Vijay More, later Mayor of Belagavi, named the home after his mother Shantai. He and co-founder Vijay Patil started in Bamanwadi, 13 km from the city, on Jamboti Road near VTU. The work has never taken a government grant. Residents are not charged. Conditions apply — because the beds are kept for people with no sons, no pension, no property, no net.

    After marriage, Vijay and Maria lived at the ashram for eight years. Their children Allan and Cheryl were born here. The family still treats the residents as their own. Maria personally cares for women who are bedridden. Cheryl, with an MBA, teaches song and dance in her spare hours and turned the courtyard into a national stage.

    On television

    India's Got Talent, then Fit India.

    Eight grandmothers walked into Yash Raj Studio as special contestants. Navjot Singh Sidhu, Malaika Arora and Shaan stood up. Host Harsh Limbachiyaa cheered. They danced Tauba Tauba and Kaala Chasma like the floor belonged to them.

    They have also been special guests on Zee TV's Sa Re Ga Ma Pa at Film City. In March 2026, Suman Mohite, 79, received the Fit India Championship at RK Beach, Visakhapatnam, from the Sports Authority of India — then did Zumba on the sand.

    Who noticed

    Actor Vicky Kaushal praised the women and shared their work. Rapper Divine and actor Jiya Shankar have commented. Visitors have included Charan Raj and Sayaji Shinde. The courtyard collects famous names the way it collects marigolds — casually, then all at once.
